Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Emma Social handles information when users use its content publishing features and connect supported social media accounts.

Information We Process

Depending on the features and permissions selected by the user, Emma Social may process account identifiers, profile information, authorization credentials or tokens, publishing preferences, video content and associated metadata.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide requested account connection and content publishing functionality, operate and secure the service, and comply with applicable platform requirements and law.

Connected Platforms

When users connect a third-party platform such as TikTok, the platform's own terms and privacy practices also apply. Users choose whether to authorize a connection and may revoke platform permissions where supported.

Sharing

Information may be transmitted to connected platforms when necessary to perform actions requested by the user. Emma Social does not sell personal information.

Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal and operational requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information.

User Choices

Users may stop using Emma Social and revoke permissions granted to connected platforms through those platforms where supported.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Emma Social develops. The current version and effective date will be published here.
